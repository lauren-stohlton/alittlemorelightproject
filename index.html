Our story

Two companions, one idea

Hi! We’re Lauren and Inga, and we had the opportunity to serve as mission companions in Florida about two years ago. We remain deeply grateful for the testimonies we gained and the people we met. We’re now both attending BYU and recently married.

Having experienced missionary life ourselves, we know that holidays, birthdays, and other special occasions can sometimes be difficult when you’re far from home. As we reflected on our missions, we felt inspired to give back to the sisters who are serving just as we once did, and that’s how A Little More Light was born.

What we do

A little extra love this Christmas

We’re starting this year by sending small care packages to sister missionaries who may be facing financial hardship, limited family support, or other difficult circumstances while serving away from home. Our hope is to offer encouragement, practical support, and a reminder of the Savior’s love. We plan to keep growing and reach sisters during other meaningful moments throughout the year, like birthdays and holidays.

The heart behind this

We know that a sister missionary’s greatest source of strength is her relationship with Jesus Christ and the spiritual support she receives from her mission leaders. Our hope is simply to offer an additional reminder of His love through small, thoughtful acts of service.

1

Prayerfully identified

Mission leaders and loved ones are in the best position to prayerfully identify sisters who could benefit, and we’re grateful for their guidance.

2

Coordinated with care

We follow each mission’s preferences for who to include, mailing address, and delivery method.

3

Delivered with love

Thoughtful care packages arrive so each sister knows she is remembered.
